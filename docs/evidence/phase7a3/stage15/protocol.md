# Phase 7A.3 Stage 15: corrected recording campaign, protocol (frozen, NOT launched)

**Status: designed, estimated and frozen; nothing has been recorded.** Recording needs the user's approval of the
plan below by its SHA-256. `tools/phase7a3_stage15.py run` refuses to start without `--approved <that SHA-256>`.
It also refuses if the plan differs from the one the code generates, if the source tree has uncommitted changes,
or (for every subset after the first) if the feasibility check has not passed.

The commit that adds this file, `plan.json`, `estimate.json`, `calibration.json` and `tools/phase7a3_stage15.py`
is the frozen source revision. If approved, the campaign is recorded from a clean worktree of that commit, and
nothing in it is edited while recording. Each run records its revision, a dirty flag and the checksum of every
participating source file (`scenario_batch.run_one`), as Stage 9 did.

## Purpose

Stage 9 missed its pre-registered coverage: 1,367 observable frames above 25 m/s within 100 m, from 62 vehicles in
20 runs, against the 2,000 / 100 / 10 it required. The cause is in the scenario generator. `scenarios.routes()`
starts all drawn traffic at least 100 m ahead of the ego, and the ego starts 40 m along a 5 km carriageway. So no
vehicle ever came from behind, and fast traffic ahead simply pulled away.

This campaign corrects the generator and adds vehicles that originate behind the ego. Its recordings would be the
first untouched evaluation data for P4: every earlier recording (Stage 1 and the whole Stage 9 campaign) has now
been used to develop P4. Those recordings are historical development and regression data. The campaign changes no
tracker, safety rule, threshold or model.

## The generator fix (`tools/scenarios.py`)

`planned_traffic(params)` is opt-in. Without a `planned_traffic` list, `routes()` is byte-for-byte unchanged
(`tests/test_scenarios.py`). Each planned vehicle gets its own vType at a fixed speed: `maxSpeed` is that speed,
`speedFactor` is at least 1, and `speedDev` is 0. It is then inserted one of two ways:

- **From behind:** at the carriageway's start (`departPos` 0, 40 m behind the ego's start), at the moment computed
  for it to draw level with the planned ego.
- **Ahead:** placed at least 110 m ahead at time 0, slower than the ego, so the ego draws level with it.

All planned traffic keeps to the ego's neighbouring lanes 1 and 3. In the ego's own lane it would only queue behind
the ego. SUMO inserts at an edge start one vehicle at a time, so vehicles from behind in the same lane depart at
least 1.2 s apart. A vehicle held back for the one before it is made a little faster to keep its arrival time; if
that would take it more than 1 m/s over its speed band, or past its class's top speed, it is left out
(`tests/test_phase7a3_stage15.py`).

## Expected coverage, estimated before recording

**Method.** A SUMO-only dry run of every configuration (`sumo_frames`): its own network, routes and seed at the
recorded 0.2 s step. The ego is a SUMO vehicle in lane 2 that accelerates from rest to its cruise speed and never
changes lane. Vehicles are counted exactly as `tools/perception_audit.py` counts T2's denominator, through the same
functions Stage 9's `coverage` uses. The sensor geometry is the audit's `visibility`: the radar fields of view,
ranges and occlusion. A vehicle is **present** in a slot when it is that slot's true vehicle (its lane, −100 to
150 m). It is **observable** when a radar has also seen it for `CONFIRM_ALLOWANCE_S`. No Webots, radar or RoadSense
runs in the dry run; it only replays the planned trajectories through SUMO.

## Adequacy, unchanged from Stage 9

On the evaluation subset: at least **2,000** observable frames above 25 m/s within 100 m, from at least **100**
distinct vehicles in at least **10** runs, and at least **200** frames above 30 m/s. Below that, coverage is
reported as insufficient, and the campaign is not extended without approval. The estimate gives margins of 5.2×
(frames), 2.1× (vehicles), 2.7× (runs) and 17.5× (frames above 30 m/s).

- Bulk data stays outside the repository, in `~/Library/Application Support/RoadSense/campaigns/phase7a3-s15-{feasibility,development,evaluation,stress}/`.

## Stopping conditions

- Every configuration is attempted once. A failed attempt is recorded and kept, never retried.
- 3 failed attempts in a row stops the subset: an environment fault, to be reported.
- Wall-time limits per subset: feasibility 15 min, development 30 min, evaluation 90 min, stress 15 min.
- A busy port fails the attempt before anything starts.
- No implementation file is changed while a subset is recording.
- **Feasibility gate.** `phase7a3_stage15.py feasibility plan.json estimate.json` checks two things after the
  feasibility subset:
  - all 4 configurations recorded successfully
  - observed frames and vehicles above 25 m/s within 100 m reach at least half the dry run's (≥ 259.5 frames,
    ≥ 12.5 vehicles)

  Its verdict is written once, beside the campaign. If it fails, recording stops and the result is reported;
  the plan is not redesigned without approval. This gate is where differences between the dry-run ego and the real
  one would show: the real ego slows behind vehicles in its lane and for cut-ins, and may change lanes to
  overtake. Vehicles driving straight in the neighbouring lanes do not trigger the controller's half-speed rule:
  its `front left/right 0` sensors look 7.5° off axis, reaching only about 1.7 m to the side within their 8 m
  trigger distance.

## Evaluation (run once, as written, after all subsets are recorded)

For each subset campaign `C`, outputs under `~/Library/Application Support/RoadSense/analysis/`:

1. `tools/tracker_replay.py verify C` checks:
   - checksums
   - contiguous 200 ms frames and server counts
   - the truth join
   - an exact replay of the live slot features
2. `replay C --tracker legacy | p1 | p1p2 | p1p2p4 | legacyp4 | oracle`. The tracker configurations are A, B, C,
   D and the LP4 ablation, exactly as committed. The oracle is the evaluation-only truth reference.
3. `tools/perception_audit.py C --slots` for each tracker, then:
   - `compare`: T2–T10 against the limits of `docs/phase7a3-plan.md` §5, unchanged
   - `judge` against the oracle
   - `confirm`
   - `diff --oracle` for each pair
4. `tools/track_failures.py trace` and `analyse` for each tracker, against both the footprint and body oracles,
   plus `expiries` for D and LP4. Then `docs/evidence/phase7a3/stage14/summarize.py` tables.
5. Coverage:
   - `phase7a3_highspeed.py coverage`
   - this campaign's recorded counts beside `estimate.json`

## Acceptance

- **Adequacy:** the four thresholds above, on the evaluation subset. Unchanged.
- **T1–T13:** each reported as pass, fail or unverified against `docs/phase7a3-plan.md` §5, with every limit
  unchanged. Acceptance statements use the evaluation subset only.
- **Pre-registered P4 comparisons.** These are additional, not changes to T1–T13, all on the evaluation subset:
  1. Real threats must not be hidden. No oracle CAUTION, HIGH or CRITICAL episode before any collision may have
     C warning while D never does; any such episode fails P4.
  2. D's unsupported HIGH and CRITICAL frames before collisions are reported against C's, under both oracles, with
     each frame that only D has classified by truth at D's threat track (Stage 14's method).
  3. Stationary-lead tracking with the ego stopped within 25 m is reported for C and D.
  4. Handover kept-track rates (front→rear and rear→front, same direction) are reported for C and D.
- Simulator results are not real-world driving evidence. Mac results are not Raspberry Pi acceptance.

## What approval covers

Approval of this SHA-256 covers recording these 48 configurations once, in the order and within the limits above,
and the evaluation as written. It does not cover:

- any tracker, safety, threshold or model change
- enabling P1, P2 or P4 anywhere live
- P3, P5 or Phase 7B
- any change on the Raspberry Pi
