# Phase 7A.3 Stage 9: targeted recording campaign, protocol (frozen before recording)

Written and committed **before any of these runs was recorded**. The commit that adds this file, `plan.json` and
`tools/phase7a3_highspeed.py` is the frozen source revision: the campaign is recorded from a clean worktree of that
commit, and nothing in it is edited while recording. Each run also records its revision, a dirty flag and the
checksum of every participating source file (`scenario_batch.run_one`).

## Purpose

The Stage 1 recordings hold 16 observable frames above 25 m/s within 100 m, all from few vehicles: too little
to test T2's top band. This campaign adds expressway traffic above 25 m/s from independent scenario groups, plus
slow and stationary vehicles and v2 cut-ins, recorded as raw sensor frames for offline replay of the three tracker
configurations. It changes no tracker, safety rule, threshold or model.

## Configurations (`plan.json`, sha256 `1c58ea740681459757b8bcf70d1a8e216860683b221929e66a36226235388444`)

Generated by `tools/phase7a3_highspeed.py plan` from fixed seeds; `run` refuses any other plan. Odd seeds have an
expressway limit of 100 km/h, even seeds 120 km/h (both carriageways). Cars may reach 36.1 m/s (130 km/h) and
motorcycles 30.6 m/s (110 km/h) with SUMO's usual speed spread (`speedFactor`); trucks (22 m/s), buses (20 m/s) and
autorickshaws (13.9 m/s) keep their top speeds and are the slow vehicles of the mix. The ego drives at 80 km/h
(cut-ins: the staging's drawn 40–80 km/h), so same-direction traffic approaches from behind and recedes ahead in
the neighbouring lanes, and oncoming traffic closes at up to ~58 m/s.

| Subset | Builder | Seeds | Runs | What |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| diagnostic | expressway | 11, 12 | 2 | traffic only (`normal` draw), limit 100 / 120 km/h |
| diagnostic | gradual, assertive, sudden cut-in | 11 | 3 | `cutin_staging.draw` v2 |
| diagnostic | stationary lead | 11 | 1 | `sudden_brake` draw braking to 0 km/h, held ≥ 6 s |
| **evaluation** | expressway | 101–114 | 14 | as above, independent traffic per seed |
| **evaluation** | gradual cut-in | 101–106 | 6 | lane change 3–5 s, observed 3–6 s first |
| **evaluation** | assertive cut-in | 101–106 | 6 | lane change 2–3 s, observed 1.5–3 s first |
| **evaluation** | stationary lead | 101–104 | 4 | a lead stopping in the ego lane |
| stress | sudden cut-in | 101–104 | 4 | lane change 1–1.5 s, 0.4–1 s in view: hard by design |

The **diagnostic** subset may be inspected first (that high-speed traffic appears, that the staging acts) and is
the only data any future tuning may use. The **evaluation** subset stays untouched until the single analysis
below. **Stress** runs are reported separately and never pooled into acceptance figures. No tracker parameter is
changed in this stage: A (legacy), B (P1) and C (P1+P2) are evaluated exactly as committed (P2's noise values from
`33bcf12`).

## Recording

- `tools/phase7a3_highspeed.py run docs/evidence/phase7a3/stage9/plan.json` from the clean worktree. Each attempt
  is `scenario_batch.run_one(..., capture=True)`: its own Webots (`--batch --mode=fast --no-rendering`), SUMO port
  8901 and RoadSense server port 8011 (live tracker: the default legacy one), never the open Webots session
  (SUMO 8873) or the development server (8000). The Raspberry Pi is not involved.
- Recorded per run: `sensor_frames.jsonl` (raw radar and sensor observations exactly as the ego sends them),
  `ground_truth.jsonl` (simulator truth, **evaluation only**; no tracker reads it), the server history, logs, and
  a source snapshot; per campaign `SHA256SUMS`, `frozen.json` (plan checksum, revision). Bulk data stays in
  `~/Library/Application Support/RoadSense/campaigns/phase7a3-s9-{diagnostic,evaluation,stress}-20261117/`.
- **Attempt limit: 40** (each configuration once). A failed attempt is recorded and kept, never retried.
- **Stopping conditions:** all 40 attempted; or 3 failed attempts in a row (an environment fault: stop and
  report); or 90 minutes of wall time; a busy port fails the attempt before anything starts.

## Expected coverage (estimated before recording)

At 100 km/h about 85% of cars and motorcycles (75% of the mix) desire more than 25 m/s; at 120 km/h nearly all.
Expected in the evaluation subset: several thousand observable frames above 25 m/s within 100 m, from hundreds of
distinct vehicles in all 14 expressway runs, including frames above 30 m/s. **Adequacy, fixed now:** at least 2,000
observable frames above 25 m/s within 100 m, from at least 100 distinct vehicles in at least 10 runs, and at least
200 frames above 30 m/s. Below that the coverage is reported as insufficient; the campaign is not extended without
approval.

## Evaluation (run once, as written)

For each subset campaign `C` (outputs under `~/Library/Application Support/RoadSense/analysis/`):

1. `tools/tracker_replay.py verify C` — checksums, contiguous 200 ms frames, server counts, truth join, exact
   replay of the live slot features.
2. `replay C --tracker legacy | p1 | p1p2 | oracle` (the oracle is the evaluation-only truth reference).
3. `tools/perception_audit.py C --slots` for each tracker; `compare` (T2–T10, limits of docs/phase7a3-plan.md §5,
   unchanged); `judge` against the oracle; `confirm` (track-level confirmation by true speed); `diff` with
   `--oracle` for legacy→P1, legacy→P1+P2 and P1→P1+P2.
4. Coverage: observable frames, distinct vehicles and runs per true speed band, within 100 m and beyond.

Acceptance statements use the evaluation subset only, with every T-criterion reported as pass, fail or
unverified. Simulator results are not real-world driving evidence.
